# HerAccess - Final Demo Video Script

**Tip for recording:** Take your time. Let the UI load before you move your mouse. The lines in **bold** are the absolute most important things you **MUST SAY** to impress the judges!

---

## 1. Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
**Action:** Show the HerAccess homepage.
**Script:** 
"Hi! Welcome to HerAccess. **When women move to a new city, finding safe and verified accommodation is extremely hard.** The information out there is fragmented and often fake. We built HerAccess to solve this using Bright Data Scraper Studio."

## 2. AI Search (0:15 - 0:35)
**Action:** Type: *"I need a safe women's hostel in Gomti Nagar under 10000"* and click Search.
**Script:** 
"Instead of checking 10 different filters, you just type what you need naturally. **Our engine parses your location and budget, and automatically anchors your safety requirements.**"

## 3. The Safety Ecosystem (0:35 - 1:00)
**Action:** Scroll through the results. Click on the "Metro Stations" and "Hospitals" tabs.
**Script:** 
"We don't just show you hostels. Safety is an ecosystem. **HerAccess automatically maps the surrounding area, showing you the absolute closest Metro stations and 24x7 support centers.**"

## 4. Quality Over Quantity (The Primary Source Flex) (1:00 - 1:15)
**Action:** Click on the "Hospitals" tab and show the Apollo or KGMU result.
**Script:** 
"Notice we don't spam the map with hundreds of unverified listings. **We use Bright Data to extract deep, authoritative data—like trauma capabilities and 24x7 hotlines—directly from primary institutional domains like KGMU and Apollo.**"

## 5. Auto-Healing Scrapers (The "Wow" Moment) (1:15 - 1:45)
**Action:** Open the Demo Panel (Bottom Right). Click "Break Scraper". Show the missing data. Then click "Heal Scraper". Wait for it to show the Bright Data CLI terminal output.
**Script:** 
"Websites change their UI all the time, which breaks normal scrapers. Watch this. I will simulate a broken website. The data validation drops to 0%. **But when I trigger a Heal, HerAccess connects directly to the Bright Data CLI. The AI automatically rewrites the scraper code on the fly to recover the data—no developer needed!**"

## 6. Conclusion (1:45 - 2:00)
**Action:** Show the Transparency Dashboard.
**Script:** 
"**By leveraging Bright Data Scraper Studio, HerAccess ensures women always have access to real, live, and verified local knowledge.** Thank you!"
